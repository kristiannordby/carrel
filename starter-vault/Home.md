# MBAn vault

Made by the Carrel installer. Open this folder in [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md) as a vault (Open folder as vault), or just use it from Carrel.

- [[planner]]: every graded deliverable and hard date for the six Fall 2026 classes. Carrel reads its tables, so keep the column layout.
- `Atlas/Resources/Lectures/<class>/`: lecture slides. Carrel saves new ones here from Canvas and links its study sets to them.
- `Efforts/Courses/<class>/`: homework, solutions, handouts and data from Canvas.
- `Efforts/Exams/`: your own cheat sheets and practice notes. Carrel shows them next to the lectures they cover.
- `Attachments/Syllabi/`: syllabi.
