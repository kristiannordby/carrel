## Standing rules

**Optimization Methods**
- **Late homework:** −10 points out of 100 **per half-day**. Two days late = zero.
- **Homework is individual.** Concept-level discussion is fine; copying, prior-year solutions, and outsourced work are not.
- **GenAI:** allowed for brainstorming, editing, debugging, explanations. Not allowed for solving assigned problems or submitting generated work as your own. **Any use must be disclosed.**
- **Attendance:** all lectures and recitations mandatory, on time, full session.
- **Midterms:** 80 min, one double-sided sheet of notes. Per updated syllabus: Midterm 1 covers through L12, Midterm 2 through L20.
- **Term project submission:** Gradescope, one submission per team (add all members). Technical report 5–10 pp; video ≤5 min, shared as a link on p.1 of the report. Report must include a short AI-use statement (e.g. appendix); undisclosed GenAI = integrity violation. Per [[Efforts/Courses/Optimization Methods/Term project description.pdf|Term project description]].

**ML Ops**
- **Late homework:** −10 points out of 100 **per half-day**, unless an extension is obtained before the due date or extenuating circumstances apply.
- **Individual work**, but discussion of conceptual issues/general ideas is fine — list who you talked to about each problem at the top of the pset. Copying = grade reduction/F.
- **GenAI:** "AskTIM" tutor available within Canvas for understanding/help (not solutions) on homework.

**Advanced Analytics Edge**
- **Deliverables are group work** (3–4 students/group); the In-Class Exam is individual.
- **GenAI:** limited use — may support understanding, must be cited; do not submit deliverable problems as prompts or paste GenAI output as your write-up.
- **Attendance:** all classes mandatory, notify teaching team of extenuating circumstances.

**Analytics Lab**
- **Data destruction is a course requirement.** Any sponsor-supplied data/documents must be destroyed at project end (approved recycling for print; secure deletion for digital). Your own final paper/derivative work (no proprietary content) can be kept.
- **NDA**: MIT and each sponsor company have a signed NDA; each team member signs an acknowledgment.
- **Team composition:** teams of 4, at least 1 non-MBAn/business-oriented member (1:3 ratio). TAs may rebalance.
- **Attendance mandatory** at Pitch Day (9/11) and Final Presentations (12/11).

**From Analytics to Action**
- **Attendance mandatory** unless extenuating circumstances (notify course TA).
- **Digital devices** restricted to class-related activities during session.
- **Teams of 5–6**, at least one non-MBAn/business-oriented member per team of 5–6.
- **AI usage in labs**: used as a "mirror and challenger," not a producer — prompts used during lab must be submitted with the deliverable.

**Hands-On Deep Learning**
- **GenAI:** unrestricted on assignments (MIT's unrestricted policy). The **exam is closed book** — no notes, internet, models or assistants.
- **Attendance:** taken at the start of each session with A-plus; counts toward the 30% participation grade.
- **Teams:** up to 4 (5 only with TA permission), for the Assignment and Hackathon.
- **Tools:** Parley API for model access ($30/month MIT credits; key at platform.parley.mit.edu) and Google Colab. Slides go up before class, notebooks after.
